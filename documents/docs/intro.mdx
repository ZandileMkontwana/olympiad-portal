# Olympiad Portal

The Olympiad Portal is a full-stack web application built to manage the end-to-end lifecycle of academic olympiads — from round creation and paper management, through online test-taking and automarking, to standings, certificates, and appeals.

## What the portal does

Organisers create olympiads and configure rounds with open/close schedules. Educators register students and track their progress. Students sit tests online with timed sessions, autosave, and resume-on-disconnect. The system automarks MCQ papers, calculates standings, and manages qualification-based advancement between rounds.

Key capabilities include:

- **Round lifecycle automation** — a scheduler ticks every 30 seconds to transition rounds through `scheduled → open → closed` states, triggering notifications and auto-advancing qualified entrants.
- **Online test-taking** — timed sittings with periodic answer autosave, resume after disconnect, and submission with exactly-once semantics under concurrent load.
- **Multi-olympiad support** — several olympiads can run concurrently, each with its own timezone, without data leaking across olympiads.
- **Co-organiser access** — multiple organisers can collaborate on an olympiad, with membership-based access control.
- **Marking and appeals** — a moderation queue for manual marking tasks, and a full appeals workflow where educators can request remarking, organisers assign reviewers, and approved appeals amend scores and re-trigger qualification in a single transaction.
- **Standings and certificates** — live standings ranked by score, per-school rankings, and on-demand certificate PDF generation.
- **Audit log and round replay** — every automatic scheduler action is logged, and organisers can replay a round's history as a timeline.
