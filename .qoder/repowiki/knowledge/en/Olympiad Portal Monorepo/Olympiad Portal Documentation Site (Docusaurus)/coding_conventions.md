- Documentation content is written as MDX files under `docs/` and automatically included in the autogenerated `tutorialSidebar` via `dirName: '.'`.
- Blog posts use MDX with frontmatter and are organized by date-prefixed directories or files under `blog/`, with author and tag metadata centralized in `authors.yml` and `tags.yml`.
- Site-wide styling is applied through a single custom stylesheet at `src/css/custom.css` referenced by the classic preset's `theme.customCss` option.
- Static assets (logos, social cards, favicons) are placed under `static/img/` and referenced by relative paths in `docusaurus.config.js`.
